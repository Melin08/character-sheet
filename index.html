    Universal Character Importer

    How to retrieve character JSON from PC on Parchment
    1. Open pconparchment.com and open your character sheet.
    2. Press F12 to open Developer Tools, switch to the Network tab, and click Fetch/XHR.
    3. Refresh (F5), click the characters request, open the Response tab, and copy all of it.
    How to retrieve character JSON from D&D Beyond
    Set your D&D Beyond character privacy to Public, copy your numeric Character ID from the URL, and open:
    https://character-service.dndbeyond.com/character/v5/character/YOUR_ID_HERE
